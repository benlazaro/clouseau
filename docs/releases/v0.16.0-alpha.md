Title: Clouseau 0.16.0-alpha — Find in the entry, quicker time ranges, filters you can see working

## Find in the entry, quicker time ranges, and filters you can see working

### Time range
- **Type a time as digits.** In the **Time** popup, digits are formatted as you type or paste them: `20261123030700` becomes `2026-11-23 03:07:00`. A space or `T` between the date and the time works too.
- **A short time is filled out for you.** Type a date, an hour or a minute and the rest shows in gray. **Tab**, **Enter** or **Apply** fills it in. **From** gets the first moment of what you typed and **To** the last, so **To** `2026-11-23` includes the whole day (`23:59:59.999`).
- **To now includes the minute you type.** **To** `10:15` means up to `10:15:59.999`. Before, it stopped at `10:15:00.000` and left out the lines logged during 10:15. Ranges saved in your session keep their old meaning until you edit them.
- **Changing the display timezone filters again.** The range is read in the timezone times are shown in. Before, changing that timezone left the rows filtered for the old one, so the rows no longer matched the **Time** button.

### Find
- **Find in the detail pane.** Press `Ctrl+F` with the focus in the detail pane, or click its search button, to search the selected entry as it's shown, formatted JSON and HL7 included. Every match is marked and the current one is boxed. `Enter` and `F3` step through them, and **Aa** and **.\*** switch on case and regex. The search stays as you move between rows, and each entry opens at its first match.
- **Find says when it wraps.** Stepping past the last match back to the first (or the first back to the last) shows a short note over the rows.
- **Find says when nothing matches.** The search field turns red, and `Enter` tells you there are no matches, or what's wrong with the pattern.
- **Reopening Find searches again**, so its marks and count come back.
- **Find keeps working after you change a filter.** Before, stepping through matches after filtering could fail with an error.

### Filters
- **A long filter or search shows that it's working.** When a text filter or Find takes more than a quarter of a second on a big file, a thin bar fills along the top of the rows and the rows fade until it's done.
- **Spaces around `|` in the message filter are ignored**, so `timeout | refused` works like `timeout|refused`.

### Plugins
- The plugin API is unchanged (0.3.0). Plugins built for 0.15 keep working.

### Upgrading
- Close Clouseau before you start the new version. On Windows, keep the old `Clouseau` folder's `data` folder (your settings, sessions, parsers and saved connections), delete the rest, and unzip the new version in its place. On macOS and Linux your data is in `~/.clouseau/`, outside the app, so replace the old app with the new one.
